Actions supported by this file

  • Call the high-risk tier within 48 hours and arrange a visit within 7 days. Do not use the score to deny care.
  • Flag two or more prior emergency visits, a diabetic medication change, and eight or more diagnoses.
  • Pharmacist review when 20 or more medications were prescribed, or when diabetic therapy changed.
  • Make specialty and weight required fields. Add admission dates, discharge disposition, cost, and satisfaction before those questions can be answered.
